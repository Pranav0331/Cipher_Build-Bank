# CIPHER

link to project : https://cipherbuildbank.vercel.app/

### Connected Intelligence for Pattern & Hidden Entity Recognition

> **Detect patterns. Trace networks. Predict financial crime.**

CIPHER is an AI-powered financial crime detection platform designed to identify suspicious activity that may remain invisible when transactions are analyzed individually.

Instead of treating every transaction as an isolated event, CIPHER models financial activity as a **dynamic network**, combining graph analysis, temporal intelligence, anomaly detection, prediction, simulation, and explainable investigation.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Financial crime often hides in the **connections between transactions and accounts**.

Traditional detection systems can struggle with:

- **Transaction Fragmentation** — small transfers can hide illicit activity.
- **Network Obscurity** — multiple accounts can conceal the movement of money.
- **Time-Based Evasion** — suspicious behavior may only become visible over time.
- **Alert Overload** — thousands of alerts can overwhelm investigators.
- **Manual Investigation** — tracing complex money flows is slow and difficult to scale.

The key question is:

> **What if we could see the crime before the network finishes forming?**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

CIPHER creates a **Financial Digital Twin** of transaction activity and continuously analyzes it to uncover hidden financial crime patterns.

The system combines:

### 1. 🔗 Digital Twin
Convert financial transactions into a dynamic graph representing:

- Accounts
- Transactions
- Money flows
- Relationships
- Network structures

### 2. 🧠 Pattern Discovery
Use AI and graph-based analysis to identify suspicious activity and emerging patterns.

### 3. 🕒 Temporal Intelligence
Analyze how entities and transaction behavior change over time.

### 4. 🕵️ Crime Reconstruction
Trace money flows from source to beneficiary and reconstruct suspicious networks.

### 5. 🔮 Prediction
Predict potential future fund movements and network changes.

### 6. 🧪 Counterfactual Simulation
Generate hypothetical scenarios to understand how suspicious networks could evolve and test potential interventions.

### 7. 📊 Explainable Investigation
Provide investigators with risk scores, suspicious connections, and explanations instead of simply generating black-box alerts.
